About us

Welcome to our nutritionist app, where health meets expertise. We are a dedicated team of certified nutritionists passionate about helping you achieve your health and wellness goals. Our mission is to provide personalized guidance tailored to your lifestyle, preferences, and needs. Whether you're looking to improve your eating habits, manage a medical condition, or simply feel your best, we are here to support you every step of the way. In our business, your goals are our priority!

Your profile gives you full control to update or view your information whenever you wish. You can easily make changes to your details, keeping your account up to date. It's designed to offer a personalized experience tailored to your preferences.

Patients can easily book appointments by selecting from a list of predefined options tailored to various needs. This system simplifies scheduling and ensures availability while providing clear choices for patients.

Creating a preliminary nutrition plan involves assessing an individual's dietary needs based on their goals, health status, and lifestyle. This plan typically includes recommended calorie intake, macronutrient distribution, and a sample meal outline. It serves as a starting point for personalized adjustments based on progress and feedback.

Patients can conveniently view their scheduled appointments through their profile. The list includes details like the date, time, and type of appointment, helping them stay organized.

A nutritionist can easily create an appointment by selecting the desired date and time for the session. The process allows for inputting essential details such as the type of consultation , control, or examination.

Nutritionists can easily access a detailed overview of all the appointments they have scheduled. This includes information on the patient, date, time, and type of appointment. The system allows them to manage their appointments efficiently, make adjustments, and ensure a seamless experience for their clients.

Nutritionists have access to a dedicated section for reviewing complaints submitted by patients. This feature allows them to view, address, and manage feedback efficiently. By understanding patient concerns, nutritionists can enhance the quality of their services and maintain a high standard of care.

A nutritionist creates a personalized nutrition plan by evaluating the client's dietary habits, health conditions, and nutritional goals. The plan is tailored to support the client’s specific needs, ensuring long-term sustainability and health benefits.

Administrators have the ability to block nutritionists if necessary. This feature ensures that any violations of policies or inappropriate behavior can be promptly addressed, safeguarding the quality and integrity of the services provided. Blocked nutritionists will no longer have access to their accounts or the platform's functionalities until the issue is resolved.

This section allows administrators to review all user-submitted complaints and the responses provided to them. Administrators can view the details of each complaint, including the patient who submitted it, the nutritionist the complaint was about, the related appointment, and the content of both the complaint and the response.

The admin has the ability to unblock both patients and nutritionists who were previously restricted from accessing the platform. This feature ensures that users who have resolved their issues or disputes can regain full access to their accounts and platform services.
